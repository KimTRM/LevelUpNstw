Bagani: Guardians of the Archipelago - Complete Game Design Document
Game Overview
Title: Bantay: Guardians of the Archipelago
Genre: Action / Co-op PvE (2–4 Players)
Theme: Filipino Mythology Meets Disaster Response
Style: Fast-paced top-down action with light tactical co-op
Platform: PC (Local or LAN Multiplayer)
Target Rating: T for Teen (Fantasy Violence, Mild Language)
Core Gameplay Mechanics
Primary Game Loop: Defend → Purify → Survive
Each round follows a three-phase structure that escalates in intensity:
Phase 1: Defend (60 seconds)
Initial Threat Assessment: Players spawn at designated safe zones across the archipelago map
Disaster Manifestation: Natural disasters begin affecting different zones (floods, fires, earthquakes, typhoons)
Corrupted Spawn Points: Dark rifts appear, spawning corrupted elementals at regular intervals
Zone Control: Players must defend civilian evacuation points while managing environmental hazards
Phase 2: Purify (45 seconds)
Altar Activation: Sacred purification altars become accessible in each affected zone
Cooperative Channeling: Requires 2+ players to stand within altar radius and channel energy simultaneously
Elemental Synergy Requirement: Specific element combinations needed for successful purification
Interrupt Mechanics: Corrupted creatures actively attempt to break channeling process
Phase 3: Survive (30 seconds)
Chaos Escalation: All remaining corrupted creatures become enraged, gaining speed and damage
Environmental Collapse: Disaster effects intensify, creating additional hazards
Final Stand: Players must survive until the archipelago stabilizes
Bonus Objectives: Optional rescue missions for extra rewards
Elemental Bantay System
Each player embodies one of four elemental guardians, each with unique abilities and roles:
🔥 Apoy (Fire Bantay)
Core Identity: Aggressive damage dealer and corruption cleanser
Primary Ability - Flame Strike: Ranged fire projectile that spreads to nearby enemies
Secondary Ability - Purifying Blaze: Creates fire barriers that damage corrupted creatures but not players
Environmental Interaction: Can extinguish water-based disasters but amplifies fire disasters
Corruption Resistance: Takes reduced damage from fire-corrupted enemies
Weakness: Vulnerable to water-based attacks and flooding zones
💧 Tubig (Water Bantay)
Core Identity: Support specialist and crowd controller
Primary Ability - Healing Rain: Area-of-effect healing that restores HP over time
Secondary Ability - Tidal Surge: Knockback wave that slows enemies and extinguishes fires
Environmental Interaction: Can stop fire disasters and create safe passages through flood zones
Corruption Resistance: Immune to drowning and takes reduced water damage
Weakness: Vulnerable to earth-based attacks and earthquake zones
🌪️ Hangin (Wind Bantay)
Core Identity: Mobility specialist and battlefield controller
Primary Ability - Gust Blast: Pushes enemies and projectiles away from target area
Secondary Ability - Cyclone Dash: High-speed movement that damages enemies in path
Environmental Interaction: Can clear poison gas clouds and redirect debris
Corruption Resistance: Unaffected by movement-impairing effects
Weakness: Vulnerable to earth-based attacks and confined spaces
🌱 Lupa (Earth Bantay)
Core Identity: Tank and defensive specialist
Primary Ability - Stone Barrier: Creates temporary walls that block enemy movement and projectiles
Secondary Ability - Earthquake Strike : Ground pound that stuns nearby enemies
Environmental Interaction: Can create bridges over chasms and stabilize collapsing structures
Corruption Resistance: Takes reduced physical damage and immune to knockdown
Weakness: Vulnerable to wind-based attacks and aerial enemies
Elemental Synergy Combos
Players can combine abilities for powerful synergistic effects:
Fire + Wind = Firestorm
Activation: Apoy casts Flame Strike while Hangin uses Gust Blast in same area
Effect: Creates expanding tornado of fire that pulls in enemies and deals massive damage
Duration: 8 seconds
Cooldown: 45 seconds
Water + Earth = Mud Trap
Activation: Tubig creates puddles while Lupa uses Stone Barrier nearby
Effect: Ground becomes quicksand, trapping enemies and reducing their attack speed
Duration: 12 seconds
Cooldown: 35 seconds
Fire + Water = Steam Screen
Activation: Both elements cast abilities in overlapping areas
Effect: Creates concealing steam cloud that provides stealth and regeneration
Duration: 10 seconds
Cooldown: 40 seconds
Wind + Earth = Sandstorm
Activation: Hangin creates cyclone while Lupa breaks stone barriers
Effect: Blinding dust storm that confuses enemies and reflects projectiles
Duration: 15 seconds
Cooldown: 50 seconds
Advanced Synergies (3-4 Players)
Prismatic Shield: All four elements combine to create ultimate defense
Elemental Convergence: Massive area damage combining all elemental types
Nature's Wrath: Environment itself becomes weapon against corruption
Enemy Types & Corruption System
Corrupted Elementals
Shadow Sprites (Basic)
HP: 50
Speed: Fast
Attack: Ranged dark bolts
Behavior: Swarm tactics, flee when isolated
Weakness: Any elemental damage

Corrupt Diwata (Elite)
HP: 200
Speed: Medium
Attack: Area corruption aura
Behavior: Summons shadow sprites, teleports when threatened
Weakness: Combined elemental attacks
Possessed Kapre (Boss)
HP: 800
Speed: Slow
Attack: Tree root entanglement, poison smoke
Behavior: Territorial, becomes enraged below 25% health
Weakness: Fire + Wind combination
Aswang Manifestation (Boss)
HP: 1200
Speed: Variable (slow/fast phases)
Attack: Shape-shifting, fear aura
Behavior: Mimics player abilities, adapts to damage types
Weakness: Requires all four elements to defeat
Corruption Mechanics
Corruption Spread: Defeated corrupted enemies leave corruption pools
Environmental Corruption: Disasters become more severe with increased corruption
Player Corruption: Taking damage from corrupted sources slowly corrupts players
Purification: Standing near altars or other players slowly removes corruption



Disaster Response System
Dynamic Disaster Generation
Each round randomly selects 2-3 disaster types that affect different map zones:
Flood (Tubig's Domain)
Visual Effect: Rising water levels, reduced visibility
Gameplay Impact: Slows non-Tubig players, conducts electricity
Player Response: Tubig can create safe paths, others must find high ground
Escalation: Water level rises over time, creating isolated islands
Wildfire (Apoy's Domain)
Visual Effect: Spreading flames, smoke effects
Gameplay Impact: Damages all players except Apoy over time
Player Response: Apoy can control fire spread, Tubig can extinguish flames
Escalation: Fire spreads to new areas, creating walls of flame
Earthquake (Lupa's Domain)
Visual Effect: Screen shake, falling debris
Gameplay Impact: Random knockdown, terrain changes
Player Response: Lupa can stabilize ground, others must avoid fault lines
Escalation: Chasms open, buildings collapse
Typhoon (Hangin's Domain)
Visual Effect: High winds, debris storms
Gameplay Impact: Pushes players and projectiles randomly
Player Response: Hangin can navigate freely, others need shelter
Escalation: Wind speed increases, visibility decreases
Disaster Interaction Matrix
Fire + Wind: Creates firestorms that spread rapidly
Water + Earth: Causes mudslides that block pathways
Fire + Water: Creates steam that obscures vision
Wind + Water: Generates waterspouts with knockback effects
Complete Control Scheme
Movement Controls (WASD/Arrow Keys)
W/Up Arrow: Move North
A/Left Arrow: Move West
S/Down Arrow: Move South
D/Right Arrow: Move East
Diagonal Movement: Supported for 8-directional movement
Movement Speed: Base speed modified by elemental type and corruption level
Combat Controls
Left Mouse Button: Primary elemental ability (direction-based targeting)
Right Mouse Button: Secondary elemental ability
Mouse Movement: Aim direction for targeted abilities
Mouse Wheel: Cycle between ability targets (enemies/environment/allies)
Interaction Controls
E Key: Interact with altars, rescue civilians, collect items
F Key: Channel/hold action for purification altars
Tab Key: Target nearest enemy or interactive object
Shift (Hold): Walk slowly for precise positioning
Communication Controls
Enter: Open text chat
V (Hold): Push-to-talk voice chat
1-4 Keys: Quick communication commands (Help, Ready, Danger, Rally)
Alt + Click: Ping location on map for team coordination
Interface Controls
Escape: Pause menu / Settings
M: Toggle map overlay
I: Open inventory/upgrade screen
L: Toggle player list and scores
F11: Toggle fullscreen mode
Advanced Controls
Space Bar: Dash/dodge ability (universal for all Bantay)
Ctrl (Hold): Modifier for precision targeting
Z: Use consumable item (healing, energy restoration)
F: Ready/unready status for starting next round
Map Design & Zones
Core Map Structure
Each map represents a different island in the Filipino archipelago with 4-6 distinct zones:
Zone Types
Barangay (Village Center): Civilian evacuation point, safe respawn area
Kagubatan (Forest): Dense terrain with limited visibility, Kapre spawn areas
Dalampasigan (Coastline): Vulnerable to floods and typhoons, fast travel routes
Bundok (Mountain): High elevation, earthquake-prone, strategic vantage points
Sakahan (Farmland): Open areas with limited cover, optimal for large battles
Templo (Temple Ruins): Contains purification altars, ancient Bantay shrines
Dynamic Environment Features
Destructible Terrain: Walls and barriers can be destroyed by abilities or disasters
Interactive Elements: Bridges that can be raised/lowered, gates that require teamwork
Environmental Weapons: Rockslides, falling trees, collapsing structures
Weather Effects: Rain affects fire abilities, wind affects projectile accuracy
Maps by Difficulty
Beginner: Luzon Lowlands
Size: Small (5 zones)
Disasters: Single type per round
Enemy Density: Low
Special Features: Tutorial markers, extended timer
Intermediate: Visayas Archipelago
Size: Medium (6 zones)
Disasters: Two types per round
Enemy Density: Medium
Special Features: Moving platforms between islands
Advanced: Mindanao Highlands
Size: Large (8 zones)
Disasters: Three types per round
Enemy Density: High
Special Features: Vertical terrain, multi-level altars
Expert: Palawan Caverns
Size: Complex (10 zones)
Disasters: All types possible, extreme intensity
Enemy Density: Maximum
Special Features: Underground passages, light-dependent gameplay
Progression & Upgrade System
Bantay Mastery Levels
Each elemental guardian has 50 mastery levels, unlocking new abilities and improvements:
Levels 1-10: Foundation
Ability Power: +2% per level
Ability Cooldown: -1% per level
New Unlocks: Basic combo tutorials
Levels 11-25: Specialization
Elemental Synergy Bonus: +3% per level
Corruption Resistance: +2% per level
New Unlocks: Advanced combos, utility abilities
Levels 26-40: Mastery
Ultimate Ability: Unlock powerful single-use per round ability
Environmental Control: Enhanced interaction with disaster zones
New Unlocks: Leadership abilities that boost team performance
Levels 41-50: Transcendence
Mythic Power: Can channel abilities of other elements at reduced effectiveness
Disaster Immunity: Complete immunity to matching elemental disasters
New Unlocks: Legendary synergy combos, transformation abilities

Gameplay Instructions & Tutorial Flow
New Player Onboarding
Tutorial 1: Basic Movement & Combat (5 minutes)
Movement Tutorial: Practice WASD movement in safe environment
Camera Control: Learn mouse-look and targeting basics
Basic Attacks: Use primary abilities against practice targets
Elemental Introduction: Choose first Bantay and learn their identity
Tutorial 2: Elemental Synergies (7 minutes)
Partner Introduction: AI or second player demonstrates complementary element
Simple Combos: Practice Fire + Wind and Water + Earth combinations
Timing Practice: Learn coordination timing for successful synergies
Environmental Effects: See how elements interact with map features
Tutorial 3: Disaster Response (10 minutes)
Disaster Recognition: Identify visual and audio cues for each disaster type
Role Specialization: Learn how your element handles specific disasters
Teamwork Basics: Practice coordinated response to multiple threats
Altar Purification: Complete first successful purification ritual
Tutorial 4: Full Round Simulation (15 minutes)
Complete Experience: Play simplified version of full defend-purify-survive cycle
AI Teammates: Computer-controlled allies demonstrate optimal play
Mistake Recovery: Learn how to adapt when plans go wrong
Victory Conditions: Understand what constitutes success vs. failure
Advanced Gameplay Tips
Team Composition Strategies
Balanced Composition: One of each element for maximum synergy options
Specialist Focus: Multiple of same element for overwhelming single-threat response
Support Heavy: Multiple Tubig players for healing-focused survival strategy
Aggressive Rush: Multiple Apoy players for fast corruption clearing
Optimal Round Strategies
Pre-Round Planning: Assign zones and responsibilities before disasters manifest
Information Sharing: Constantly communicate disaster locations and intensity
Resource Management: Balance ability cooldowns with threat priorities
Adaptive Response: Switch strategies based on random disaster combinations
Mastery Techniques
Animation Canceling: Interrupt ability animations for faster response
Predictive Positioning: Anticipate disaster spread patterns
Combo Chaining: Link multiple synergies for extended battlefield control
Emergency Protocols: Develop backup plans for worst-case scenarios
