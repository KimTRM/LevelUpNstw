# Bantay: Guardians of the Archipelago

When natural disasters strike the islands, ancient spirits known as “Bantay” awaken to protect the people — but corrupted creatures born from chaos threaten to destroy everything.

Players become the Bantay — each representing an element of nature (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth) — and must cooperate to seal rifts and defeat corrupted elementals before time runs out.

Think Overcooked meets Hades with a Filipino twist.

---

### Core Gameplay Loop

1. Defend → Purify → Survive

- The map has zones affected by natural disasters (floods, fires, quakes).
- Players must move fast between zones, fighting corrupted spirits and performing quick cooperative tasks (like channeling energy together to purify an altar).
- Each round gets faster, with new threats spawning — teamwork and timing decide if the archipelago survives.

2. Elemental Synergy System

- Each Bantay has one main skill:  
   🔥 Apoy – spreads flame but can purify corruption faster  
   💧 Tubig – slows enemies, puts out fires  
   🌪️ Hangin – knocks back mobs, clears poison gas  
   🌱 Lupa – raises walls or barriers for protection
- Combining abilities creates synergy combos (Fire + Wind = Firestorm, Water + Earth = Mud Trap).
- Encourages team strategy in real time.

3. PvE, not PvP

- Avoids overlap with last year’s party/tag entries but keeps action intensity.
- AI enemies escalate each wave, giving an Esports-like survival pressure that’s easy to spectate.

---

### Visual & Feel

- Art Style: Stylized 2D/3D hybrid, glowing elemental designs, top-down camera.
- Mood: Mythic and heroic, like a modernized Filipino folklore world.
- Sound: Drum beats, kulintang tones, and elemental SFX.\*\*
